Round-based tactical FPS in three.js. You and two VANGUARD ally bots face four WARDEN foe bots on Kestrel Yard, an industrial map with three lanes and two bomb sites (A Crane Bay, B Tank Farm). Plant or defuse the charge, manage money across rounds, buy weapons and gear.

- Rifle HARRIER-7, shotgun BREACH-12, grenade SPARK-9, plate vest, ammo refill
- Bots that patrol, hold, rotate, plant, and defuse; fixed-step simulation
- First to 5 rounds, max 9; buy phase, bomb timer, match reset

Play it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080.
